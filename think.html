MAKE THE REASONING INSPECTABLE

What would change your mind?

Start with observations and constraints you can justify. Keep assumptions visible. A statement does not become a fact because it feels fundamental.

A worked example: a room cools overnight

Observation: A thermometer records a fall from 20°C to 16°C in two hours after heating stops. Check calibration and measurement location.

Model: A simple heat balance: C dT/dt = P − H(T − T_out). C is effective heat capacity, P is heating power and H describes heat loss. This assumes roughly uniform indoor temperature and approximately constant conditions.

Unknown: How much loss comes from air leakage versus conduction? The temperature trace alone may not separate them.

Test: Compare cooling with and without a temporary draft seal, recording outdoor temperature and wind. Predict a slower cooling rate if leakage is important. A smaller change than expected would challenge that explanation.
