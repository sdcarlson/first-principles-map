## Consequential design decisions

1. Three domain records: Target, Attempt, Assessment. Exact artifact bytes remain native files addressed by digest. Observations and interpretations are separate text fields on an attempt; neither becomes accepted truth on submission. An external target may be recorded before its checker is registered, but can only receive non-acceptance assessments.
2. A target version fixes scope and acceptance. A submission names a base snapshot and target digest. Changed assumptions require another target; stale submissions require explicit reconciliation. Same request identifier plus identical content is retry-safe; conflicting reuse is rejected.
3. The local store is single-writer, append-only through the tool and inspectable through Git. It is not tamper-proof against its owner. Store record reads and evidence consumption verify digests; package manifests provide integrity inventories, not signatures. No silent overwrites, adjudication by vote, or automatic scientific inference. Actor names are local labels, not authentication. A real runner must enforce the authority boundary outside the worker’s writable directory.
4. The submission interface accepts data and native evidence only. It cannot choose code, commands, checker settings, timeouts or acceptance thresholds. A pinned trusted checker runs separately and parses bounded JSON; arbitrary submitted code is never executed by this increment. This avoids claiming a plain subprocess is a security sandbox.
5. A mechanical check is a result, not the entire assessment. The reviewer names what it establishes and what it does not, and records usefulness separately. Preserve challenge, narrow, withhold and superseding assessments. A disputed dependency requires re-review; it does not become false by propagation.
6. Relevance selection is explicit and coverage is disclosed. Include the chosen attempts, their dependencies and all associated assessments, including objections. “No matching record” never means “untried” or “impossible.” Changed-scope retries are permitted with a reason.
7. C and D start from one curated content set: the Markdown note is readable directly, with a complete evidence appendix; the structured version stores exactly those sections and links, with equal raw-history access. Matching content digests are tested. Prose quality and completeness still require a blind human content audit before research runs; this implementation does not certify that its note is an excellent real-study baseline.
8. Four conditions are prepared with the same target, external information, capabilities, resource limits, submission and checker. A has no previous internal history; B has raw history and full-text retrieval; C adds a curated note; D adds the same curated content in structured records. History arms retain only their own chain. Missing costs remain unknown, never zero.

## Experiment contract, before outcomes

The local deterministic demonstration is a **fixture for software mechanics**. It cannot estimate the hypothesis, constitute an excellent real research handoff, establish demand, or validate cross-model behavior. A fresh process with only exported inputs is a context-reset mechanics test, not a fresh-model experiment.

Entry to a real experiment requires one identified domain owner, original histories with permission to use them, a trusted independent checker, a usefulness rubric, a curation reviewer, blinded outcome reviewers and a budget owner. The first four real tasks test handoff feasibility without their authors. Stop and repair if authors must reconstruct unavailable evidence or explain most handoffs.

Retain the synthesis’s scale: 12 sealed tasks, two blocks, four conditions, three successive fresh-context workers = 96 chains and 288 episodes. Balance all six orders of three distinct model families across the 24 task blocks; use matching orders across conditions. Do not count episodes as independent tasks. A possible extension to 24 tasks must be declared before the initial runs and requires continued observed need; one extension only.

Before opening later outcomes, freeze task/history/search/checker digests, exact model identifiers and settings, runner version, tokens/tool/verifier/compute/wall-time caps, equal curation ceilings, labor rate and cost allocation, order randomization seed, usefulness units, exclusion rules, retry cases, and the statistical estimator. These task-specific values cannot be invented without a partner and budget. Fixture caps never authorize model spending.

Primary estimator: within each task and arm, sum distinct accepted useful units across its two blocks and divide by total allocated cost. Estimate paired mean task differences in units/dollar, with a paired task-cluster bootstrap (resample whole tasks retaining every arm/block/episode) and percentile 95% interval. Report aggregate arm rates too. Relative decision gates compare paired mean arm rates. Define an absolute minimum difference with the domain owner before runs for any zero baseline; no infinite ratios. Task-cluster intervals are a bounded screen, not a claim of adequate power.

Count compute dollars plus all human preparation, curation, review, correction and maintenance time at the preregistered rate. Charge shared task preparation equally to all arms; charge representation-specific work to its arm. Preserve common experiment engineering separately and include allocated totals and sensitivity. Report minutes and dollars separately and sensitivity to labor rate and setup amortization over 1, 5 and 20 **actual** handoffs. Unknown bills or labor invalidate an economic result until completed.

Progress units must be exact-checker accepted, useful under a blind preregistered rubric, and absent from frozen history. The same construction/result earns one unit per chain, regardless of how many claims describe it. Replication can be valuable without counting as new progress. Track actual duplicate executions, known-error recurrence, unsupported acceptance, false prerequisite reuse, valid approach diversity, wrongly suppressed retries, first useful-check time, author interventions and checked dependency depth. Labels requiring judgment need named blinded reviewers and an adjudicator; no automatic truth score.

### Pass only if all synthesis gates hold

- D improves progress/cost at least 30% over C and 50% over B; its task-clustered 95% lower bound over C is positive.
- Avoidable duplicate execution falls at least 30% versus C, while total progress and valid-approach diversity each fall no more than 10%.
- No accepted false prerequisite causes downstream acceptance; unsupported acceptance is no more frequent than C.
- Benefit is positive on at least 8/12 tasks and at least two of three receiving model families.
- Extra capture/review costs pay back within five actual handoffs. The three-episode screen alone cannot certify this; collect actual reuse before product promotion.

Stop structured product work if the upper 95% bound is below 10%, or the point estimate is nonpositive with at least 20% more expert labor. Repair before another trial if a false prerequisite causes downstream acceptance or harmful exclusions suppress more than 10% of otherwise valid progress. Crossing zero is inconclusive. If C captures the benefit and D adds none, keep the note template/exporter and remove unnecessary structured maintenance. A technical pass licenses a narrow adoption test, not a public platform.
